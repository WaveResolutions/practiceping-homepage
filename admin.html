How a practice goes from trial to paid: review the signup → create the trial practice → send the payment link. The client enters a card once. They are not charged until the trial ends, then $199 is charged automatically every month and the practice switches to Active on its own. A failed payment shows as "Payment failed". Use Mark active only for a client who pays you outside the card system.
Who can do what. Everyone signs in with their own email, password and authenticator code, and every change is recorded in Activity.
  • Owner: everything, including this Team tab, cancelling accounts, marking accounts active and viewing tax IDs.
  • Manager: reviews signups, creates and edits practices, extends trials, sends payment links.
  • Support: read-only, plus resetting a practice user's password.
