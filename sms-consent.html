SMS Consent Details

PracticePing · WaveResolutions LLC · Last updated: September 28, 2026

This page documents how PracticePing (operated by WaveResolutions LLC) collects patient consent before sending any SMS messages. Consent is always optional and never a condition of care. PracticePing only messages patients who have affirmatively opted in through one of the channels below.

1. Paper Intake Form (Primary)

The primary consent method is an optional SMS section on the patient intake form, completed at the point of care before or at the time of the patient's visit. See the full form template: practiceping.com/sms-consent-form.html

Form language (SMS consent checkbox)

"I agree to receive text messages from PracticePing (operated by WaveResolutions LLC) at the mobile number I have provided above, about my visits to [Practice Name]. Messages may include requests for feedback after my appointment, reminders to schedule a check-up if I am overdue, and notices when a same-day appointment opens up. Message frequency varies (up to 5 messages per month). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of receiving care."

The patient checks the opt-in box, writes their mobile number, and signs. The signed form is retained by the medical practice per their records retention policy. Practice staff record the consent date and their initials in the "Office Use Only" section.

2. Verbal Consent (Staff-Collected)

When a patient calls to schedule or arrives at the front desk, staff may collect verbal consent using the following script:

Staff verbal script

"Would you like to receive text messages from PracticePing at your mobile number about your visits here at [Practice Name]? You'd get up to 5 messages a month — things like a follow-up after your appointment, a reminder if you're due for a check-up, or an alert if a same-day slot opens up. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help. Saying yes is completely optional and has no effect on your care."

Staff record: patient response (yes/no), mobile number, date, and their own name. Records are retained by the practice.

3. Web Form (Online Appointment Request)

Patients requesting an appointment online via the practice's website encounter an optional, unchecked checkbox:

Web form checkbox label

"I agree to receive text messages from PracticePing at my mobile number about my appointment request with [Practice Name]. Messages may include appointment confirmations, follow-up requests, and scheduling reminders. Message frequency varies (up to 5/month). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not required to request an appointment."

The checkbox is unchecked by default. The patient must actively check it to consent. The web form also links to our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Opt-Out / Help

  • To opt out: Reply STOP, CANCEL, or UNSUBSCRIBE to any message. No further messages will be sent.
  • For help: Reply HELP or INFO, or email info@waveresolutions.com.
  • Mobile numbers are never shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Sample Messages

Review request

PracticePing for [Practice Name]: Hi [First Name], thank you for your visit. If you have a moment, we'd love your feedback: [link]. Reply STOP to opt out.

Check-up reminder

PracticePing for [Practice Name]: Hi [First Name], it's been a while since your last visit. Ready to schedule your next check-up? Call us at [phone] or reply YES and we'll reach out. Reply STOP to opt out.

Same-day appointment opening

PracticePing for [Practice Name]: Hi [First Name], a [Time] appointment just opened up today. Reply YES to claim it or call us to schedule. Reply STOP to opt out.
