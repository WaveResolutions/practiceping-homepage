Review Outreach

Every patient visit is a review you haven't asked for yet.

After each appointment, PracticePing sends a personalized SMS or email — from your practice's own domain — with one tap to your Google review page. Warm, brief, or personal templates. You choose what goes out.

  • Emails sent from reviews@yourdomain.com — not a generic platform address
  • Three message tones: warm, brief, personal
  • Bulk-send all eligible patients at once, or one at a time
  • HIPAA-validated — no patient health data in messages

Lapsed Patient Recovery

Your best patients stopped coming. Here's how to bring them back.

PracticePing identifies patients who haven't visited in 12 or more months and sends a warm, personal re-engagement message. No cold outreach — these are patients who already trust your practice.

  • Automatically flags patients lapsed 12, 18, or 24+ months
  • Sends via SMS or email — whichever they have on file
  • Tracks re-engagement status: contacted, booked, opted out
  • Bulk or individual outreach

No-Show Slot Recovery

A cancelled appointment doesn't have to mean an empty slot.

When a patient cancels, PracticePing immediately notifies patients on your waitlist. They respond, you confirm, and the slot fills — often within the hour. No phone calls needed.

  • Instant SMS notification to waitlist patients when a slot opens
  • Patient replies YES to claim — you confirm from the dashboard
  • Tracks fill rate and estimated revenue recovered
  • Works alongside your existing scheduling system
